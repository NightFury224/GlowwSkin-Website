GlowwSkin

Computational dermatology imaging, powered by a phone.

GlowwSkin turns a smartphone into a dermatology imaging tool. A conical imaging chamber clips over the phone's rear camera and carries a precision-aligned macro optic and controlled multispectral lighting. A RAW capture pipeline feeds AI analysis of texture, pigmentation, redness and pore distribution.
