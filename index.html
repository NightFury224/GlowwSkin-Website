How it works

From event to evidence in four steps.

Every record follows the same guarded path: captured, sealed, extracted and verified, with no back door along the way.

STEP 01

Capture

Events from connected systems stream into the Cyber Black Box server as they happen.

STEP 02

Seal

Each entry is sealed on write, so any later change, removal or reordering becomes detectable.

STEP 03

Extract

The Raspberry Pi 5 node pulls logs out over a secure pathway. Nobody plugs into the box directly.

STEP 04

Verify

Extracted logs are checked against their seals, and any sign of tampering is flagged straight away.

Edge extraction node

Raspberry Pi 5, configured to say no.

Log extraction runs on a purpose-configured Raspberry Pi 5 that acts as the only door to the black box, and keeps that door shut to anything it doesn't trust.
