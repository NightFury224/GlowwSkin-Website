How it works

From log to extraction in four steps.

Logs are recorded, watched by tamper detection, moved through secured pathways and extracted by a Raspberry Pi 5 that blocks unauthorized devices.

STEP 01

Capture

Logs from connected systems are recorded by the Cyber Black Box server.

STEP 02

Protect

Tamper detection watches the stored logs.

STEP 03

Move

Logs travel through secured pathways.

STEP 04

Extract

A Raspberry Pi 5 extracts logs securely and blocks unauthorized devices.
